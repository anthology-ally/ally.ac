Take accessibility beyond the classroom: Blackboard Ally for Web

The same high expectations that students have for learning content also applies to your website. Ally for Web supports accessible online content, ensuring that students can interact with important information along their learner journey.

Alternative Formats

Adding Ally to your website allows key content to be downloaded in a range of alternative formats, including audio, ePub, braille and more. Automated translations are also supported for 70+ languages, which is vital for international students and other non-native speakers.

Insights and Reporting

Just as Ally provides in-depth data for accessibility in the LMS, the same rigor is also applied to your website. Ally’s Website Accessibility Report provides granular detail on how your website performs for accessibility, all based on WCAG 2.2 Level AA standards, with easy export options to integrate this data into your institution’s accessibility strategy.
